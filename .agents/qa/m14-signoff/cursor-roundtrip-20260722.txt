## Note on the "sync stall" false alarm
An early snapshot showed the queue growing (93k→241k) with "only 1 ingest POST", read as a stall.
That was WRONG — it caught (a) the one-time enqueue burst of the initial Cursor poll (76k items)
before the drain caught up, and (b) the first sync batch still in flight. The ingest log then showed
steady `/v1/ingest` 200s every ~5 s (~500 items/batch), the queue began draining (241k→227k), and
Cursor events flowed into the archive (0→32k). The sync is HEALTHY; the backlog is a large one-time
drain, not a stall.
